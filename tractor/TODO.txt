- Add an account system so that players can easily join and rejoin as the same person, as the current system of rejoining is a bit jank. When first on the website, players should be given the option to play as a guest or to login/create an account. To rejoin a game you were in and left, simply log in to your account and you should be sent back to the game you were in. This account system should persist even through server restarts and whatnot.
- The number of players in a lobby should not be fixed, instead there should be an option to lock more players from joining.
- If a player joins the lobby in the middle of play, they are added to a spectator list, displayed under the score on the left sidebar. They are able to watch the game and have the option to watch from each player's perspective with buttons, and also type in the chat. They will then be present in the lobby for the next game.
- The resize bar no longer changes the size of the cards. implement a solution that both resizes the cards and is responsive.
